Why parents choose us

More than lessons: a foundation for life

Qualified teachers

Trained, caring staff who know each child by name and plan lessons around how young children learn best.

Pupil-centred learning

Children learn by doing: exploring, asking questions and working together, at a pace that suits them.

Music, Dance, Drama & Sports

We nurture every talent, on stage and on the field, so children grow in confidence and teamwork.

Safe & secure campus

A secure, spacious environment where children can play freely and parents can relax.

Meals & welfare

Porridge for nursery, a lunch programme for primary, and a school matron caring for health and hygiene.

Guidance & counselling

Teachers support each child's feelings and behaviour as well as their learning, working closely with families.
