Our story & vision

Nurturing the development of every child with care, quality education and love.

Who we are

A place for growth

Established with a passion for early childhood development, Kamuli Hill Infant School has been a place of learning in Kireka for families who want the best start for their children.

We believe that every child is unique and deserves an environment that brings out their individual talents. Our teachers follow a pupil-centred approach, so children learn by exploring, asking questions and doing.

Our motto

“Nurture the development of a child”

What we stand for

Every child, every day

Three promises guide everything we do for the children in our care.

Holistic curriculum

Reading, numbers and early science alongside music, dance, drama and sport, so children develop in mind, body and character.

Safe & secure environment

A secure, spacious school where children are supervised, comfortable and free to play.

Experienced caregivers

Trained teachers, nannies and a school matron who look after each child's learning, health and happiness.
