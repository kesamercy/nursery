A neighbourhood school for little ones

We teach children from Day Care to P.2 on Kireka Kamuli Road. Here's who we are and what a year with us looks like.

Who we are

Small children, taken seriously

We only teach young children, from Day Care to P.2. Everyone here, from the teachers and nannies to the matron, is here for little ones.

Our teachers follow a pupil-centred approach. Children learn by touching, counting, asking questions and trying again. In nursery that means a lot of play with a purpose. In P.1 and P.2 it means reading, writing and numbers, with textbooks provided.

We also believe every child has something they're good at. For one it's reciting a poem, for another it's a traditional dance or a part in the school play. We make sure they get the chance to find it.

Our motto

“Nurture the development of a child”

What we stand for

Three things we promise every family

The basics, done properly

Reading, writing and numbers come first. Music, dance, drama and sport sit alongside them, because a confident child learns better.

A safe place to be little

A walled, spacious compound with grass to run on and a slide to play on, where children are supervised and free to play.

Care beyond the classroom

Trained teachers, Day Care nannies and a school matron. Between them, they look after your child's learning, meals, health and feelings.

A year at Kamuli Hill

More than the classroom

Lessons matter most. These are the days children talk about when they get home.

Play days on the compound

Mats go down on the grass and out come the balls, blocks, toy keyboards and telephones. It looks like play, and it's how little ones learn to share, count and talk to each other.

Sports day

Sports day opens with a march-past. Pupils in caps and sashes parade across the compound carrying flags. It teaches discipline and teamwork, and the children are proud to take part.

Music, Dance & Drama Production

A highlight of the school year. Pupils take a real stage in a hall full of families to recite, sing, act in plays and dance, from ballet to traditional Ugandan dances.

Who looks after your child

Administration

Academics

School Matron

Welfare
