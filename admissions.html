Join our family

We are accepting applications for Day Care, Baby Class, Middle Class, Top Class, P.1 and P.2.

How to apply

Four simple steps

  1. Call or visit

    Call 075 891 6432 or drop by the school on Kireka Kamuli Road to see our classrooms.

  2. Bring the requirements

    A photocopy of your child's birth certificate and two recent passport photos.

  3. Pay the admission fee

    The admission fee is 50,000 UGX, paid when you register your child.

  4. Start school

    We'll welcome your child into their new class and help them settle in.
